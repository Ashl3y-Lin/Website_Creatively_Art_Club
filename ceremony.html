Award Ceremony & Art Gallery

Our first Creative Challenge open to other schools: one night, four schools and one art gallery.

The night of the ceremony

For our 4th annual Creative Challenge, we opened the competition to other schools for the first time. On the night of the ceremony, we turned the Ossi Room into an art gallery, welcomed families, judges and guests, and announced the winners from all the schools.
